Engineering principles

Credibility is part of the architecture.

01

Evidence before assertion

Technical claims should be measurable, reproducible, and tied to an identifiable record.

02

Operational context matters

A trustworthy model still depends on reliable data, services, people, and response processes.

03

Share what can be reused

Public artifacts should use synthetic data and avoid employer code, confidential systems, and proprietary details.
